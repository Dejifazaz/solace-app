Acquisition Playbook

Updated Aug 1, 2026 · Property Acquisition

Standard process for evaluating and calling on a target property, from first contact to close.

1

Sourcing

Pull new landlord and agent leads weekly. Prioritize properties matching current acquisition criteria (unit count, area, condition).

2

The Call

  • Confirm ownership and intent to sell or lease
  • Log the call immediately with the correct status
  • If they need time — mark Will Get Back To Us and follow up in 5 business days
  • If a time is agreed — mark Callback with the date/time in notes
3

Moving to Target

Once interest is confirmed, add the property to the Pipeline as a Lead with an ARV and repair estimate. Move it through Contacted → Negotiating → Under Contract as the deal progresses.

4

Daily Targets

Every team member is expected to hit the daily call target set in OKR Goals before end of day. Progress is tracked live on the Property Acquisition dashboard.

Rule of thumb

If a lead sits in "Will Get Back To Us" or "Callback" for more than 3 days without a follow-up, it shows on the Follow-Ups Due list — check it first thing every morning.
