Operations Handbook

Updated Jul 28, 2026 · Operations

Maintenance escalation, vendor contacts, and inspection cadence for owned properties.

1

Maintenance Escalation

Non-urgent requests are logged as a Task and assigned within one business day.

Urgent requests

No heat, no water, or safety issues get called in to the on-call vendor immediately — do not wait for a Task to be assigned.

2

Inspection Cadence

Every owned property is inspected quarterly. Schedule inspections as Tasks with a due date and the assigned team member.

3

Vendor Contacts

Preferred vendors are kept in the CRM tagged by trade. Always confirm current rates before dispatching a job.
