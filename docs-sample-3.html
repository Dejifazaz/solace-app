Brand & Messaging Guide

Updated Jul 15, 2026 · Marketing

Voice, tone, and visual guidelines for Solace campaigns and outbound materials.

1

Voice & Tone

Direct, calm, and credible — we're talking to landlords and agents who've heard every pitch. No hype, no urgency tactics. Lead with specifics: numbers, timelines, and what happens next.

2

Visual Identity

Sage green and near-black on white. The wave mark stays clear of other elements — don't crowd it with text or crop it tightly. Keep layouts uncluttered; whitespace is part of the brand.

3

Campaign Checklist

Before anything goes out, confirm it against the current acquisition targets so messaging and pipeline stay aligned.

Before you publish

Check the Acquisition Funnel on the Main Hub — campaigns should target whichever stage is converting worst that month.
